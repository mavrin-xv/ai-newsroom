# The Ultimate Guide to Closing Your Books Faster With a Seamless Month-End Process

Month-end close is the process of reviewing, reconciling and finalising a company's accounts at the end of each month. For small service businesses it is often slow and painful.

## TL;DR

- Receipts
- Bank matching
- A checklist
- Software helps

## Step 1: Stop chasing receipts at the end

The shoebox is where month-end time goes. In our pilot, 31% of expense lines had no receipt on the first day of close. Every one of those is a message to a colleague, a wait, and a reminder.

Set one rule: a receipt is photographed the day the money is spent, or the card is paused. It sounds harsh. It works because the cost lands on the person who can fix it.

## Step 2: Match the bank weekly, not monthly

Doing the bank match once a month means four weeks of lines at once. Weekly, it is a 20-minute job, and the odd line is still fresh in someone's memory.

Pick a fixed slot, Tuesday morning for example, and match everything that cleared since the last slot. Anything you cannot match goes on a short list with a name next to it.

## Step 3: Use one checklist, in the same order, every month

```
[ ] all receipts in
[ ] bank matched to the last day
[ ] payroll journal posted
[ ] accruals for unbilled work
[ ] accountant's list sent
```

The order matters. Payroll and accruals depend on a clean bank match, so doing them first means doing them twice. Most teams that close in 7 working days follow a list like this one.

## Step 4: Send the accountant's list on day 2

Your accountant cannot start until they have your questions. Send the list on day 2 with what you know and what you do not, instead of on day 6 with everything finished.
