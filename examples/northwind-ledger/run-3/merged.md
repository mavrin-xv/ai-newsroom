## Recommended (13)

1. line 23 "Put the receipt count on the same Tuesday slot as the bank match in Step 2." (format)
   - issue: A step in a follow-in-order guide depends on a later step the reader has not reached yet.
   - fix: format: Move the Tuesday-slot instruction into Step 2, or say in Step 1 only that a weekly check comes in Step 2.
2. line 8 "One pilot firm spent 9 working days on its month-end close." (claims)
   - issue: Proof supports 9 working days only as the median across 14 firms, not as one firm's figure. The scan marks it sourced via a sources file I cannot see.
   - fix: claims: Cite the source for this specific firm, or reword to the median pilot firm.
3. line 8 "The owner saw the month's numbers on calendar day 10, a third of the way into the next month." (claims)
   - issue: No source. 9 working days from the 1st ends on calendar day 11 at the earliest, so day 10 does not reconcile unless the close starts before month end.
   - fix: claims: State when the close clock starts, or correct the calendar day, and name the source.
4. line 8 "None of the four changes requires new software." (claims)
   - issue: Unsourced absolute not in the Proof section.
   - fix: claims: Back it with the pilot report or soften to a claim the draft can support.
5. line 8 "the median close went from 9 working days to 5 after four changes" (claims)
   - issue: Proof gives the before and after medians, not that the four changes caused the drop. The title and description repeat this causal framing.
   - fix: claims: Confirm attribution in the pilot report or word it as before and after, with the four changes as what the firms did.
6. line 12 "the chasing behind the 31% of lines that had no receipt." (claims)
   - issue: Drops the scope in Proof: pilot customers' expense lines on day 1 of close. Reads as a general rate.
   - fix: claims: Add "in our pilot, on day 1 of close" or link the figure here.
7. line 19 "the shoebox was where a lot of month-end time went" (claims, voice)
   - issue: Time spent on receipts is not in Proof, which measures missing receipts only. Also vague.
   - fix: (pick one)

8. line 33 "Two lines will keep coming back: card refunds that land weeks after the purchase" (claims)
   - issue: Stated as universal fact with no basis.
   - fix: claims: Add "in our experience" or cite the pilot.
9. line 45 "Payroll and accruals depend on a clean bank match, so doing them first means doing them twice." (claims)
   - issue: Unsourced technical claim that carries Step 3's argument for the order; payroll journals often do not depend on the bank match.
   - fix: claims: Give the concrete dependency or frame it as the team's practice.
10. line 8 "Across the 14 firms in our" (icp)
   - issue: First screen does not say who the firms are, so the target reader cannot confirm the advice is for a small service business like theirs.
   - fix: icp: Add one clause in the opening paragraph naming the segment: small service businesses of 10 to 60 people still closing in spreadsheets.
11. line 42 "accountant's list sent (it goes out on day 2, before the lines above are done)" (icp, voice, format, claims)
   - issue: The checklist is sold as 'same order every month', but this line is listed last and sent on day 2. The reader cannot tell when to tick it.

     - claims: State the unit once, in Step 4.
12. line 57 "Under a few dozen bank lines a month, in our judgement" (voice)
   - issue: Vague, unsourced number; she always gives the exact figure with its source.
   - fix: voice: Count your bank lines for one month first. If the count is small, a weekly match is overhead; we did not measure a cut-off.
