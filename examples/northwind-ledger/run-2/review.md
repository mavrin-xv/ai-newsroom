- Item 5, line 12: claims wanted the vague "most" replaced with a pilot figure or dropped; voice offered the 31% rewrite. Both agree on direction; I took voice's wording because the 31% is in Proof and the sentence stays true.
- Item 10, line 31: icp, voice and claims all raised it. Voice's fix has a placeholder "N" and "300 lines" that are not in Proof; claims accuracy beats the pretty sentence, so I took the honest "not measured" version.
- Item 13, line 57: voice proposed "40 bank lines". That number is not in Proof or `sources.md`, so claims accuracy wins; the threshold stays rounded and is marked as judgement.
